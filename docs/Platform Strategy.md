# Platform Strategy

## Positioning

Reselling OS is a mobile-first operating system for small reselling teams. It replaces scattered notes, photo rolls, listing drafts, and spreadsheets with one auditable flow from intake through sale and realized profit.

The current application is the reference workspace: it proves the workflow with a real operation before it is offered to other businesses.

## Ideal first customer

A small but active resale business with one owner and one to five operators. They need a shared inventory workflow, photos from iPhone, clear listing status, repeat-stock handling, and simple sales records; they do not need autonomous marketplace posting or enterprise accounting.

## Product promise

- Capture inventory from a phone without losing work.
- Keep real units, reusable listings, and sales history separate and accurate.
- Let teams collaborate without exposing the owner's costs or profit.
- Make repeat stock and inbound shipments understandable instead of spreadsheet-heavy.

## Platform principles

- Each customer business is a hard security boundary.
- Financial privacy is enforced in the data layer, not only hidden in the interface.
- Marketplace actions remain human-controlled unless a supported API proves otherwise.
- AI proposes structured work; a person reviews and owns the business record.
- New features must improve the essential loop before adding spectacle or automation.

## Go-to-market sequence

1. Finish the multi-business tenant migration and platform reliability gates.
2. Run a closed beta with a handful of trusted resellers.
3. Observe onboarding, weekly usage, first inventory intake, first sale, and support friction.
4. Refine the minimum paid plan around the workflow customers actually use.
5. Open paid access only after data isolation, billing behavior, and support procedures are proven.

## Measures that matter

- A new business completes first intake and reaches a ready listing.
- Operators return weekly to manage real inventory.
- Sales are recorded without financial corrections or duplicate inventory.
- Invited teammates collaborate without seeing restricted financial data.
- Support requests expose a concrete onboarding or workflow improvement.

## Non-goals for launch

Do not market autonomous buying, guaranteed profit, automated Facebook posting, tax advice, or broad marketplace intelligence before the underlying data and integrations are trustworthy.
